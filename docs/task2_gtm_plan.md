# Wilmarcs: Australian Go-to-Market Plan (Task 2)

*Prepared 11 November 2026. Goal: replace referral-only work in Australia with a repeatable pipeline for a Sydney team that can take about 6 new projects a quarter.*

---

## 1. Two segments

### Segment A: ASX-listed mid-caps that publish sustainability or impact reports

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Why they need video** | The Sustainability Report is a dense 60–120 page PDF that few staff or investors read. FY26 is the **first year of mandatory AASB S2 climate disclosure** for Group 1 entities with 30 June year-ends, so these companies have just published new, technical content that needs explaining. A 2–3 minute film and cut-downs gets it used (AGM opener, investor day, careers page, LinkedIn). Greenwashing scrutiny favours a studio that scripts from the published figures. |
| **Who signs off** | Head of Sustainability (owns the story, often the budget) with the GM Corporate Affairs or Head of Communications (approves spend). Legal or the Company Secretary checks claims. In smaller mid-caps, one Head of Marketing & Communications covers all of it. |
| **Typical budget** | **AUD 12,000–40,000** for a flagship impact film (2–4 shoot days across sites, interviews, cut-downs). **AUD 8,000–12,000** for a single-site film built from the report. Basis: Sydney 3-person crew at roughly AUD 3,000–5,000 a day plus edit time. This sits within comms budgets that already pay for report design. |
| **Best time to reach** | **Aug–Nov**, after the report is released (Aug–Oct) and through AGM season (Oct–Nov). **Feb–Mar**: after half-year results, while they plan the FY27 report, so filming can happen before 30 June. **May–June**: EOFY use-it-or-lose-it spend. Avoid mid-Dec to late Jan. |

### Segment B: Event and conference organisers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Why they need video** | A highlight film sells next year's tickets, sponsorships and exhibitor space. Sponsor contracts often include filmed deliverables, and session capture becomes on-demand content. |
| **Who signs off** | Event Director (owns the event P&L) and Marketing Director (owns promotion). The Managing Director at owner-led SMEs; the Head of Marketing or Partnerships at festivals. |
| **Typical budget** | **AUD 5,000–12,000** for a one-day highlight film. **AUD 12,000–35,000** for multi-day multicam coverage with session capture, interviews and same-day social edits. We filter for multi-day events, which clear the AUD 8,000 floor. |
| **Best time to reach** | **8–12 weeks before the event**, when crews are booked: late Jan–Mar for the **Feb–Jun** season, May–Jul for the **Aug–Nov** season. Budgets are set per event, so the event date matters more than EOFY. |

### Where to start: Segment A

Start with **Segment A**. Each project is worth roughly twice an event job, the buying moment is public, dated and comes once a year, and the first round of AASB S2 reports gives every target new content that needs explaining. CSR and impact film is also what sets Wilmarcs apart. In events we would be competing on price with many local videographers. **Sequencing:** run A from now until about 12 December, then open B in late January for the March–June conference season.

## 4. Getting the rules right

**Spam Act 2003.** These emails are commercial electronic messages, so they need consent, sender identification and a working unsubscribe. **Consent:** we rely on *inferred consent through conspicuous publication*. We only email addresses the company publishes itself (website, report, ASX announcement), where there is no "no unsolicited messages" statement and the pitch relates directly to the person's role, such as a sustainability film for the Head of Sustainability. We never buy, harvest or guess addresses (the Act bans harvested lists), and we log where each address was published, because the sender must prove consent. **Identification:** every email names the sender and Wilmarcs and gives the ABN and Sydney address. **Unsubscribe:** a free reply-or-link opt-out that works for at least 30 days after sending, honoured immediately and always within 5 business days, and suppressed in all future campaigns.

**India–Australia time difference.**

- **AEDT (UTC+11), 4 Oct 2026 to 4 Apr 2027:** Sydney and Melbourne are **5.5 hours ahead of IST**. Under **AEST (UTC+10)** the rest of the year, they are **4.5 hours ahead**. Brisbane has no daylight saving.
- **Sending:** aim for 8–10am Sydney time, Tuesday to Thursday. That is **2:30–4:30am IST** in summer (3:30–5:30am in winter), so Bengaluru approves drafts during its day and uses **scheduled sending** in the recipient's time zone.
- **Calls:** 2–5pm Sydney time in summer (**8:30–11:30am IST**) and 1–5pm in winter (8:30am–12:30pm IST). The Sydney team covers morning calls.

**Australian calendar dates that affect timing (2026–27).**

- **30 June EOFY:** use-it-or-lose-it spend in May–June, new budgets from 1 July.
- **Reporting:** full-year results in August, half-year results in February, reports Aug–Oct, AGMs Oct–Nov.
- **Shutdown:** last sends around 12 Dec 2026, light restart around 12 Jan, full cadence after **Australia Day (Tue 26 Jan 2027)**.
- **Easter 2027:** 26–29 Mar. **Anzac Day:** 25 Apr.
- **State holidays (no sends):** VIC Labour Day (8 Mar), King's Birthday (14 Jun), VIC AFL Grand Final eve (late Sep), NSW Labour Day (first Monday in October), Melbourne Cup (first Tuesday in November).
- **School holidays** (Dec–Jan, April, July, late Sep–Oct; dates vary by state) slow replies, so don't start new sequences in those weeks.

## 5. Assumptions

1. The Sydney entity has an ABN and street address for the email footer.
2. Outreach is drafted in Bengaluru, and the Sydney team shoots and takes meetings. Post-production can be shared with Bengaluru to protect margin.
3. Budgets are estimates from Sydney day rates and the brief's AUD 8,000–40,000 band, not quotes.
4. "Mid-cap" means roughly ASX 300 outside the top 50: often Group 1 for AASB S2, but without a big in-house content team.
5. Wilmarcs has at least two CSR films it can share as case studies.
6. Nothing is sent as part of this assignment. All copy is draft only.
