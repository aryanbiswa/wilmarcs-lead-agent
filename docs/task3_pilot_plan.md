# Wilmarcs Sydney: 30-day outbound pilot

**Goal:** a repeatable path to the Sydney team's capacity of **6 new projects a quarter** (AUD 8,000–40,000 each), using timing-signal outreach instead of referrals alone.

## Targets: working backwards
| Stage | Rate assumed | Per quarter | Per week |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| New prospects emailed | | 1,200 | 92.3 |
| Emails sent (3-step sequence) | × 2.92 | 3,505 | 270 |
| Replies | 8% of prospects | 96.0 | 7.4 |
| Interested replies | 50% of replies | 48.0 | 3.7 |
| Discovery calls | 50% of interested | 24.0 | 1.8 |
| **New projects** | 25% of calls | **6.0** | 0.5 |

6 ÷ 25% = 24.0 calls → ÷ 50% = 48.0 interested replies → ÷ (8% × 50%) = 1,200 prospects over 13 weeks. That is ~54 emails a weekday, so 2 warmed inboxes at ≤30 a day.

## Tools
- **Lead research agent (this repo):** a weekly run on ~138 companies. About 2 in 3 return a sourced, dated signal and a draft.
- **Instantly:** sending, warm-up, inbox rotation, reply tracking and unsubscribes. **HubSpot CRM (free):** the pipeline. **Calendly:** 15-minute calls on Sydney time.

**Keeping emails out of spam:**

1. Use a separate sending domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, warm it up for 2–3 weeks, and send no more than 30 a day per inbox.
2. Write in plain text with no images or attachments and at most one link, and turn open tracking off on the first touch.
3. Use only verified, role-relevant published addresses, keep bounces under 2%, and honour unsubscribes immediately.

## Every Monday: 3 numbers
1. **New prospects emailed** (target 92 a week)
2. **Interested-reply rate** (target 4%)
3. **Discovery calls booked** (target 1.8 a week)

## Top 2 risks
1. **Not enough signal-qualified companies.** 92 a week is more than two segments in two cities produce with fresh signals. *Response:* add a third segment (universities or NFPs), add ASX announcements and venue calendars (ICC Sydney, MCEC) as sources, and re-check "no signal" companies monthly.
2. **Replies land below 4%**, from deliverability problems or being seen as an offshore studio. *Response:* check inbox placement first, A/B test subject lines, lead with the Sydney office and 2 local case studies, and add a LinkedIn touch before the follow-up.

## 30-day timeline
- **Week 1:** domain and inbox set-up and warm-up, CRM stages, a research run on 150 companies, and 5 hand-edited drafts approved.
- **Week 2:** first 50 sends to the strongest fits only (score ≥ 75), with replies read daily.
- **Weeks 3–4:** ramp up to the weekly target, then review the 3 numbers and reset the assumptions with the real rates.

### Before / after: Symal Group
**Before (tool):**
```
Hi there,

Symal published its first year of mandatory climate-related financial disclosure within the FY26 Annual Report on 26 October 2026 [1]. As a diversified construction and services business based in South Melbourne [2][3], this disclosure is a strong point to share your progress through a CSR and impact film that brings the report's stories to life by showing your on-site teams delivering sustainable projects.

Could we grab a 15-minute call in the next two weeks to discuss this?

[Your name]
Wilmarcs Sydney

Not relevant? Reply 'no' and I won't follow up.
```
**After (edited):**
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Symal's FY26 Annual Report includes your first mandatory climate disclosure under AASB S2 [1]. That's a lot of careful work that most clients and future staff will never read.

Wilmarcs makes CSR and impact films for listed companies. For Symal, I'd film two or three of your crews on live sites, showing the sustainability work behind the numbers, as a 2-minute film plus social cut-downs. Projects typically start from AUD 8,000.

Worth a 15-minute call in the next two weeks?

[Your name]
Wilmarcs Sydney

Not relevant? Reply 'no' and I won't follow up.
```
**What changed and why:** Removed '26 October 2026': it was the web page's date, not the report's release date, and the evidence quote doesn't contain it, so the claim wasn't backed by its source. Swapped the generic 'bring the report's stories to life' for a concrete shoot plan (crews on live sites, 2-min film + cut-downs), added an AUD 8,000 anchor so the call self-qualifies, a first-name merge tag, and a subject that names the AASB S2 moment.

### Before / after: Orora
**Before (tool):**
```
Hi there,

Orora celebrates 50 years of can manufacturing at Revesby on 19 October 2026 [1]. Milestones like this in packaging solutions [1] make it a natural moment to capture your history, your people, and what is next.

We produce corporate anniversary films that show your journey and team on screen. Based in Hawthorn [3], we would love to help you tell this story.

Are you open to a brief 15-minute call in the next two weeks?

[Your name]
Wilmarcs Sydney

Not relevant? Reply 'no' and I won't follow up.
```
**After (edited):**
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Congratulations on 50 years of can manufacturing at Revesby [1]. Half a century at one site is a rare story, and the people on that floor are the best ones to tell it.

Wilmarcs is a corporate film studio with a Sydney crew, so shooting at Revesby is simple. We'd make a 2-3 minute anniversary film: long-serving staff, the line today and what comes next, plus cut-downs for LinkedIn and careers. Projects typically start from AUD 8,000.

Open to a 15-minute call in the next two weeks?

[Your name]
Wilmarcs Sydney

Not relevant? Reply 'no' and I won't follow up.
```
**What changed and why:** The tool wrote 'Based in Hawthorn [3], we would love to help', which reads as if Wilmarcs is in Hawthorn (it's Orora's head office), and pitched a company anniversary when the milestone is one site's. Reframed it as the Revesby site's 50 years, used our real advantage (a Sydney crew near Revesby), gave a concrete shot list and an AUD anchor, and cut the subject to 4 words.
